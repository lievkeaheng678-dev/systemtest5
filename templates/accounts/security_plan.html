Security plan · ផែនការការពារសុវត្ថិភាព

1. Authentication · ការផ្ទៀងផ្ទាត់អត្តសញ្ញាណ

Passwords are hashed by Django (PBKDF2) and checked against four validators: minimum length 8, common-password list, numeric-only, and similarity to user data. A username is locked for 15 minutes after 5 failed logins.

2. Authorization · ការកំណត់សិទ្ធិ

Access is granted through groups: Administrator Staff Manager Member. Sensitive pages use @permission_required; every other page uses @login_required.

3. Sessions · សម័យប្រើប្រាស់

Sessions are stored in the database, expire after 30 minutes of inactivity or when the browser closes, and use HttpOnly and SameSite cookies. Logout is POST-only and protected by CSRF tokens.

4. Auditing · ការតាមដាន

Logins, logouts, failed logins, registrations, profile edits and account activation changes are recorded with IP address and browser. The log is read-only in the admin panel.

5. Production hardening · ការត្រៀមសម្រាប់ផលិតកម្ម

With DJANGO_DEBUG=0 the site forces HTTPS, secure cookies and HSTS. The secret key and allowed hosts come from environment variables.
